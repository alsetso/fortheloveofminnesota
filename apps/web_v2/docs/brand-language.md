# For the Love of Minnesota — Brand language

Voice rules for welcome, in-app copy, and neighbor-facing prompts.

## Who you’re talking to

A Minnesotan. Neighbor. Resident. Someone who lives here.

Speak **to** them. Not about yourself. Not about a brand character.

You are not the hero of the sentence. They are.

## What we ask of them (the three jobs)

Every story points at one of these. Say them as actions.

1. **Stand together** — Share what you love. Know your block. Check in on neighbors.
2. **Work together** — Share your services. Connect locally. Use tools to connect. Find local meetings. Show up.
3. **Share resources** — Tools to connect, congregate, and meet. Pass them along.

If a line doesn’t help them do one of those three things, cut it.

## Voice

**Do**
- Plain words. Short sentences. Direct asks.
- Second person: *you*, *your town*, *your neighbors*, *around you*.
- Speak life into them — name their strength, their place, their next step.
- Warm and clear. No performance.
- Light Minnesota place when it serves the reader (town, winter, lake, block) — never as costume.
- End each story with one definitive action they can take.

**Don’t**
- First-person autobiography (*I’ve lived here sixty years…*).
- Brand personality theater or grandpa monologue.
- Corporate fog: *leverage*, *ecosystem*, *empower*, *unlock*, *platform*.
- Soft hedges: *maybe*, *consider*, *feel free to*.
- Cleverness that outshines the ask.

## Story mode (welcome sheets)

Each card is a short story **about them**, not about us.

Structure:
1. **Title** — the action (*Stand together*, *Work together*, *Share resources*)
2. **Summary** — one hard line
3. **Beats** — 2–4 short sections that put them in the picture
4. **Ask** — one clear closing action

No emojis.

How-it-works controls live inside **Share resources** after the story.

## Product words (keep these)

- Search Minnesota  
- Find me  
- Boundaries  
- Map layers / Map style  
- People / Addresses  
- Your route  
- Come on in  

## CTA & sharing

- Primary button: **Come on in**
- One share line near the CTA: *Sign in with email. Then tell a neighbor.*

## Tone checks

1. Is this about the reader — not the narrator?
2. Would they know the next step in one breath?
3. Does it serve stand / work / share?
4. Is every word plain?

If yes — ship it.
